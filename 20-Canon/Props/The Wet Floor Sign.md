# The Wet Floor Sign

## What it does in the world

Nothing. It has been there longer than [[Vic]].

## What it does for the brand

It is the object that proves the room is the same room. A viewer who notices it
in clip four of an unrelated episode has been converted from a scroller into an
audience, which is the only conversion this account is actually running.
