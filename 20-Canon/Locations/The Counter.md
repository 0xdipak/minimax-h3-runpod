# The Counter

## Why this room exists

It is the only place [[Vic]] can be. Half the series happens in this frame, and
the audience learns it well enough that a single object out of place reads as an
event.

## Geography

Register at frame-left, lottery dispenser frame-right, door behind camera —
which is why nobody is ever seen entering. The cooler run in [[Aisle Three]]
is off to the right, deeper into the store.
