# Audio System

## The bed

Every clip carries the same bed: fluorescent hum, compressor cycle, traffic
through glass. It is appended to every prompt automatically. The bed is a
continuity device as much as an atmosphere — a viewer recognizes the store with
their eyes closed.

## What a shot may add

One sound. A door, a can, a shoe on wet tile. If a beat needs two sounds to
work, it is two beats.

## Music

There is no score inside the world. Any music is a track laid over the finished
post — a room decision, recorded in the episode's `post.sound`, never a prompt.

## Dialogue capture

Dialogue is written as if caught by a camera mic across a room: slightly too
quiet, no proximity effect, ambience never ducking under it.
