# Voice and Tone

The look is enforced by the compiler. The voice is enforced by the room. This
page is the standard the room holds each other to in notes.

## The register

One paragraph. How does this channel sound when it's working?

## Rules for dialogue

- Word budget per clip. A 10-second clip holds roughly eighteen spoken words.
- What this channel's characters never do with language.
- The move that reliably lands here.

## Rules for `action:`

The `action` field is the only writer's prose that reaches the model, so it is
written for a machine: physical verbs, nameable objects, one event per clip,
no interiority, no adjectives of mood. Mood lives in [[Visual-System]] and is
applied automatically.
