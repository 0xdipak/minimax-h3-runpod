# Audio System

## The bed

The sound appended to every prompt on this channel. It is a continuity device
as much as an atmosphere — the audience should recognize the channel with their
eyes closed.

## What a shot may add

Keep the budget explicit. One sound per clip is usually right; if a beat needs
two, it is two beats.

## Music

Whether music exists inside the world, or only as a track laid over the finished
post via the episode's `post.sound`.

## Dialogue capture

How voices sit against the bed.
