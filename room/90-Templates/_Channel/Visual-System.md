# Visual System

The `look` block in [[{{title}}]] is the machine-readable version of this page.
This page is the reasoning, so that when someone proposes changing a line they
can see what it was holding up.

## Why this stock / format

What it buys in the first half-second of a scroll.

## Why this lighting

Reproducibility argument: which instruction comes back the same every time.

## Why this palette

What anchors the eye, and which continuity token the room plants deliberately.

## Why the camera behaves this way

Aesthetic reason, and the coherence reason.

## What a writer may specify

Framing, height, whether the frame holds. Not stock, grade, or palette —
those are settled here and inherited.
