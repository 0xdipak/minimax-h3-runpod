# Do Not Render

The `negative` list in [[{{title}}]] ships on every prompt from this channel,
merged after the network's floor. Here is why each entry is there — so nobody
removes one without knowing what it was protecting.

## Adding a negative

An entry belongs here only if it is true of *every clip this channel will ever
make*. Anything narrower goes on the character, location, or shot that
conditions it — those merge into the same AVOID line at compile time, without
constraining everything else forever.
