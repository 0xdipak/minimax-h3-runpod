# A Single Egg

## Why this one

First episode of [[Nothing Happens]], so it has to teach the format in forty
seconds: the ask, the flat refusal, the worse idea, the hold. The object is
deliberately the most ordinary thing in the store — if the format works on an
egg, it works on anything, and everything after this can get stranger.

## Room notes

- The original fourth beat had Vic ringing it up for free. Cut. It resolved,
  and the whole series depends on not resolving.
- Clip three is the only one that leaves [[The Counter]], and it exists mostly
  to plant [[The Wet Floor Sign]] in episode one so it can pay off later.
- If this performs, the follow-up is one shoelace.
