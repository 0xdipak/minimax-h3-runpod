# Aisle Three

## Why this room exists

The counter is where people talk; the aisle is where people are alone. Any beat
that needs someone unobserved happens here.

## Geography

Coolers on the left, oil shelf on the right, [[The Counter]] behind and to the
left, out of frame. The aisle reads as a corridor, and the darkness at the end
of it is doing work — nothing in the brand has ever come out of it, which is
precisely why it holds attention.
