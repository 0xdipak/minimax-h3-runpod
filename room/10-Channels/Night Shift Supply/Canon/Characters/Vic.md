# Vic

## In a sentence

The night clerk, who has worked this shift so long that nothing arriving through
the door is new information.

## Where he came from

He was the day manager. He asked for nights. Nobody in the room has decided why,
and we should keep it that way — the moment it's answered it stops being funny.

## What he can and can't do on camera

Never leaves the counter in the same clip he speaks in. Never raises his voice.
Never runs. If a beat needs Vic to hurry, the beat is wrong.

## Plays against

[[The Regular]] — who needs things. That's the whole engine.
