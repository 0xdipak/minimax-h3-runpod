# The Regular

## In a sentence

She is here every night, she has never bought anything, and she is always
mid-sentence.

## Where she came from

Unestablished, deliberately. She has no outside — the store is the only place
the audience will ever see her, and that is the joke that pays off slowly.

## What she can and can't do on camera

Never crosses behind the counter. Never sits. Never seen arriving; she is simply
in the frame at the top of the clip, which is why her shots almost always open
mid-action.

## Plays against

[[Vic]] — who needs nothing.
