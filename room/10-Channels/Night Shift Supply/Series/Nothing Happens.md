# Nothing Happens

## The shape

Four beats, one per clip, and the fourth is not a resolution:

1. **Ask.** [[The Regular]] arrives mid-sentence, needing a specific object.
2. **Refuse.** [[Vic]] establishes, without moving, that the store does not have it.
3. **Escalate.** The Regular proposes a substitute that is worse.
4. **Hold.** Nobody wins. The frame stays on whoever did not speak last.

## What makes an episode belong here

The needed object has to be *nameable and slightly wrong* — plausible enough that
a real store might stock it, specific enough that this one obviously doesn't.
That single choice carries the whole episode, which is why the room spends most
of its time there and almost none on dialogue.

## What would break it

- Vic having the thing.
- A third voice.
- Any beat that requires either character to leave frame.
- Ending on a resolution. The fourth clip is a hold, not a punchline; the
  comments section supplies the punchline, and that's what drives the format.
