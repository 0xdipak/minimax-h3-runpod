# Do Not Render

The `negative` list in [[Brand]] ships on every prompt. Here is why each entry
is there — so nobody removes one without knowing what it was protecting.

| Negative | What it protects |
|---|---|
| drone or aerial shots | The bystander camera. An aerial has an author. |
| lens flare added in post | The look is optical, not decorated. |
| orchestral or swelling score | The world has no score. See [[Audio-System]]. |
| anyone smiling at camera | Rule 3. Acknowledging the frame breaks the brand. |
| daylight exteriors | Rule 1. The brand only exists between 1am and 5am. |
| text or logos in frame | Rendered type is where models fail most visibly. |
| slow motion | Reads as advertising. This is not advertising. |
| shallow depth of field on wides | Undoes the 28mm bystander geometry. |

## Adding a negative

An entry belongs here only if it is true of *every clip the brand will ever
make*. Anything narrower goes on the character, location, or shot that
conditions it — those merge into the same AVOID line at compile time, without
constraining everything else forever.
